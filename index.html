Appointment Standard — Interest alone is not enough.

Every accepted appointment must meet one client-facing status: Qualified Lead - Confirmed Appointment

  • The prospect matches the campaign's approved targeting criteria
  • The contact is the appropriate decision-maker or agreed representative
  • The prospect understands which company they will be meeting
  • The prospect understands the purpose of the conversation
  • The prospect demonstrates genuine relevance or interest
  • The prospect explicitly agrees to a specific meeting date and time
  • The applicable time zone is established
  • The prospect's contact information is captured accurately
  • The appointment is not a duplicate

If a prospect does not agree to a specific meeting date and time, the opportunity is not delivered as a qualified lead.
